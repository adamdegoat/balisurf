Bali Surf

A left-hand reef break in Bali, like Uluwatu and Padang Padang: the wave peels to your left as you ride it. Sit in the lineup, turn and paddle for a wave, and ride it with your right thumb: slide left and right to carve, like a steering wheel. On the left, hold PUMP for speed and STALL to brake and get barrelled.

For full screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and play from the icon.
